## What it does

The assistant asks questions until the problem is clear. Fresh reviewers then challenge the idea from different points of view, such as cost, ease of use, and long-term upkeep. The reviewer viewpoints are written for software ideas.

## When to use it

Use Brainstorm when you have an idea but not a plan.

## What you get

A short brief in `concepts_brief.md`. Read it, ask for changes, and continue to [Draft](/skills/egm-draft) when it is right.

## Example

```text
/egm-brainstorm
Help students book a shared study space.
```
