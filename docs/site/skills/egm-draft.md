## What it does

The assistant writes a software design document one section at a time. It covers the problem and goals, the technical plan, the alternatives, and the implementation steps. It stops after each section for your feedback.

## When to use it

Use Draft after [Brainstorm](/skills/egm-brainstorm), or when you already have notes that describe what you want to build.

## What you get

A design document in `design_draft.md`. Continue to [Review](/skills/egm-review) when the document is complete.

## Example

```text
/egm-draft
```
